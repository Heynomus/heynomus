# Product guide

Heynomus is a research workspace for following projects on Robinhood Chain.

## Discover projects

Explore the project catalogue, browse narratives, and search by name, ticker, or contract address. Each project record brings its linked sources and collected observations into one place.

## Follow the work

**Latest ships** highlights counted development events. **What changed** follows changes in the public record. **Builder radar** helps you find projects with recorded development activity. These views describe the evidence Heynomus has collected, with source dates and coverage limits.

## Read a project record

Inspect identity, public links, development activity, contracts, and available market observations. Check both the original sources and the questions that remain unanswered. A team's description, a release, and an onchain observation provide different kinds of evidence.

## Compare projects

Bring records side by side to compare the evidence and coverage. Missing information stays visible instead of becoming an invented measurement.

## Keep your research together

Sign in with Google, email, or a wallet through Privy. Save projects to your watchlist, keep private notes, and manage your workspace from your account.

## Ask project questions

The research terminal uses public project evidence to answer questions. Read the cited sources and their dates alongside the response.

## Research from X · Live

Reply to a project post with `@heynomus check this project`. Include its exact Robinhood Chain contract or official website. Heynomus replies to completed requests with a concise summary and a link to the public evidence report.

Browse [X Research reports](https://heynomus.xyz/x-research) or read the [X Research guide](X-RESEARCH.md).

## Submit a project

Use **Submit project** on the website to provide official links and context. Submissions are reviewed before publication.
