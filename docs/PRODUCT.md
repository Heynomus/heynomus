# Product guide

Heynomus is a research workspace for following projects on Robinhood Chain.

## Discover projects

Explore the project catalogue, browse narratives, and search by project name, ticker, or contract address. A project record brings its linked sources and collected observations into one place.

## Follow the work

**Latest ships** highlights counted development events. **What changed** follows changes in the public record. **Builder radar** helps you explore projects with recorded development activity.

These views describe the evidence Heynomus has collected. They do not claim to capture everything a team has done.

## Read a project record

Look through a project's identity, public links, development activity, contract context, and available market observations. Check the source dates and the questions that remain unanswered.

A project description can be a team's own claim. A release, contract observation, or dated source record provides a different kind of evidence.

## Compare projects

Bring project records side by side to compare the available evidence and coverage. Missing information stays visible rather than becoming an invented score or measurement.

## Keep your research together

Sign in to save projects to your watchlist and keep personal notes. Your saved research belongs to your account.

## Ask project questions

The research terminal helps you explore project questions using the available public evidence. Read the cited sources and their dates alongside the response.

## Submit a project

Use **Submit project** on the website to provide the project's official links and context. Submissions are reviewed before publication.

## Research from X

X Research is being prepared. It will let people request a project check within an X conversation and follow the supporting evidence on Heynomus. Availability will be announced through the official account.
