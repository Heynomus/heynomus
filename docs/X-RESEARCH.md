# X Research

X Research is live on [@heynomus](https://x.com/heynomus).

## Request a check

Reply beneath a project's post with `@heynomus check this project`. Include its official website or exact Robinhood Chain contract address to make the identity clear.

Completed checks receive a short reply with a link to a public report. The report keeps the evidence, source dates, and unanswered questions together.

## Read the full record

Open [X Research](https://heynomus.xyz/x-research) to browse published reports or search by report ID, project name, or contract. Reports link to original sources and, when matched, the project's Heynomus record.

An unavailable source stays unavailable. A report can describe partial evidence without establishing that a contract is secure or a product is working.

## Public requests and controls

Requests made in public X conversations produce public reports. Private workspace notes are not part of that research context.

Use `@heynomus stop` to opt out and `@heynomus start` to resume.
